Why I Do It

I fly to capture what can't be felt from the ground. Movement, emotion, and story in motion. There's a perspective that only exists from above, and it changes everything. A winding racetrack becomes a ribbon of speed. A wedding venue transforms into a storybook setting. An event becomes an experience you can relive.

From FPV action shots that put you in the pilot's seat to smooth cinematic aerials that reveal the bigger picture, my goal is simple: to make people feel like they were there, and remember it. Every flight is a chance to show your world in a way nobody's seen before.

About KDA

FAA Part 107 certified, fully insured, and equipped with professional-grade gear. I specialize in high quality aerial video and photography, and with a background in video production, editing, and audio/music production, I don't just capture footage. I craft complete visual stories ready for your audience.

Whether it's marketing content that stops the scroll, real estate tours that sell the lifestyle, event coverage that captures every angle, or commercial projects that demand polish, I deliver cinematic visuals designed to make an impact.
